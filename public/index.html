    How the scale works

    Grade points are awarded for each semester grade, not for the yearly average. A full-year course has two semester grades; a one-semester course has one. Each semester is worth half of the course's credits, so a two-credit course weighs double.

    For a course still in progress, the semester grade is the average of its quarters, rounded to a whole number. Anything below 70 earns no grade points.
