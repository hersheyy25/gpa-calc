    Behold the Grade Scale

    Grade points are earned by each semester grade — a year-long course is two of them, a one-semester course one — and each semester is worth half of the course's credits. Quarters are averaged into the whole semester grade your report card shows. Any grade beneath a 70 yields no grade points.
